# Private Owner Checklist: Affiliate Applications and Product Validation

> **Keep this file private.** It is for the Gear Guru Guide owner and is not a public website page, navigation item, or brand directory entry.

## Launch Sequence

Build and review the site **before** you apply. Then publish the website, connect the domain, keep the disclosure and policy pages live, and add the initial original articles. Apply to merchant programs before placing tracked links. A merchant’s approval, program terms, and link-generation tools—not a product’s presence in the research catalog—determine whether a link can be activated.

| Order | Owner action | Keep public site status |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | Publish the site, connect the domain, and activate a monitored contact address. | Original editorial research only. |
| **2** | Apply to the verified direct merchant and network programs below. | No live tracked links until approved. |
| **3** | Save each approval email, program terms, brand restrictions, and generated tracking links. | Add the required disclosure before or next to each monetized recommendation. |
| **4** | Confirm a product is active in the approved merchant catalog before linking it. | Never manually copy volatile retailer prices, ratings, stock, or reviews. |
| **5** | After a full article and video are live, activate the relevant approved link. | Clearly label sponsorship, gifted products, or endorsements when applicable. |

## Amazon Validation Queue

Amazon search results can change and browser access to Amazon is restricted in this research environment. Therefore, do **not** label anything “highest selling” from a generic search result. Review the following in your own Amazon account or, after acceptance, with approved product-data tools; capture the date and source for every decision.

| Search phrase in Amazon | Candidate topic | Validation to record |
|---|---|---|
| `wearable exoskeleton` | Hypershell X Ultra; AstroShell-type outdoor exoskeleton listings | Confirm explicit Best Seller / category rank, seller, condition, price, return terms, and current availability. |
| `robotic exoskeleton legs` | Outdoor powered-hike assist systems | Confirm model authenticity and do not convert manufacturer claims into medical or endurance promises. |
| `satellite phone Iridium` | Iridium Extreme, Iridium 9555, Iridium GO! exec, and current satellite plans | Confirm whether the listing is a device only, bundle, activation plan, or accessory; explain plan costs separately. |
| `satellite communicator hiking` | Garmin inReach, ZOLEO, SPOT, and other messengers | Verify subscription requirements and use safety-first language—no rescue or coverage guarantees. |
| `portable power station 2000Wh` | EcoFlow, Jackery, BLUETTI, Goal Zero, and DJI power systems | Verify capacity, input, weight, warranty, retailer identity, and current listing status. |
| `thermal monocular outdoor` | Premium backcountry observation systems | Confirm legal use, jurisdictional restrictions, and manufacturer support; avoid making hunting or safety claims. |
| `roof top tent` | High-end overlanding shelter systems | Confirm fit compatibility, shipping, installation requirements, and retailer warranty. |

## Video and Disclosure Rules

For every YouTube review, mention material connections in the video and description when they exist. A sponsored video, a gifted product, and an endorsement must be clearly labeled. A brand inquiry or payment must never change the review conclusion, and an Amazon or merchant link should appear only after its associated program is approved.
