# High-End Affiliate Path Research

## Verified Direct Merchant Path

Hyperlite Mountain Gear publicly operates an affiliate program through AvantLink. Its official affiliate page identifies an application process, an affiliate manager, 30-day cookie duration, and direct product examples including the Southwest backpack and Mid 1 tent. The catalog can therefore use a **direct merchant path—application required** for selected Hyperlite products after the owner is approved and receives tracking links.

Source: Hyperlite Mountain Gear, “Affiliate Program,” https://hyperlitemountaingear.com/pages/hyperlite-mountain-gear-affiliate-program.

## Verified Outdoor Exoskeleton Paths

Hypershell publishes an official affiliate program through Impact for its outdoor X Series. DNSYS also publishes an Impact-powered affiliate application page. The DNSYS page states that publishers apply, receive a review within 4–5 business days if a fit, then receive affiliate links after approval. Both brands can therefore appear in the catalog as **direct merchant path—application required**. Do not use a referral link, discount code, or merchant claim until the owner has accepted the applicable terms and received the approved publisher links.

Sources: Hypershell, https://hypershell.tech/en-us/pages/affiliate-program; DNSYS, https://dnsys.ai/pages/affiliate.

## Verified Navigation and Imaging Paths

DJI’s official publisher page is titled “DJI Affiliate Program—Share, Earn, Enjoy!” and presents a promoter-account application path. Garmin publishes an official affiliates page for media and creators. These support a **direct merchant path—application required** status for the respective DJI and Garmin research entries. As with every other catalog item, no tracked URL will be shown until the publisher accounts are approved and the owner provides the generated links.

Sources: DJI, https://u.dji.com/; Garmin, https://www.garmin.com/en-US/ambassadors-and-affiliates/affiliates/.

## Verified Retailer Paths

Backcountry provides an official affiliate-program page, and its public search result describes an application route for a premium multi-brand outdoor catalog. The browser page itself did not expose readable content in the research environment; therefore do not assign a live Backcountry link to a product until the owner is approved and confirms the product is currently carried.

REI’s official “Become an REI Co-op Affiliate” page indicates application through Impact. This is a useful secondary retail path for eligible premium products after the owner is approved and confirms product availability in the account catalog.

Sources: Backcountry, https://www.backcountry.com/info/affiliate-program; REI, https://www.rei.com/help?a=Become-an-REI-Co-op-Affiliate---id--SnToxfZRRI-o6A7lkasoWA.

## Verified High-End Power Merchant Paths

EcoFlow publicly advertises an affiliate program on its United States site. Its published program page describes a free application route and a commission structure, so EcoFlow DELTA Pro 3 and related high-capacity systems can use a **direct merchant path—application required** after the owner is approved and obtains tracked links. Jackery and BLUETTI also publish official affiliate-program pages, permitting the same non-public status for premium expandable power systems after approval.

Sources: EcoFlow, https://us.ecoflow.com/pages/affiliate-program; Jackery, https://www.jackery.com/pages/affiliate-program; BLUETTI, https://www.bluettipower.com/pages/affiliate-program.

## Verified Premium Shelter and Camp Paths

Sea to Summit’s official affiliate page states that approved AvantLink publishers can post tracked Sea to Summit links and receive commissions. YETI’s official affiliate page describes its Impact-managed publisher program and the tracking links and product assets made available after participation. The shelter and camp entries in the final 40-item list are therefore limited to Hyperlite Mountain Gear, Sea to Summit, and YETI products—brands with an official documented publisher route.

Sources: Sea to Summit, https://seatosummit.com/pages/affiliate-program; YETI, https://www.yeti.com/affiliate.html.

## Verified MSR and BioLite Paths

Cascade Designs’ official publisher page states that content creators, publishers, bloggers, product-review sites, and social sites can apply through AvantLink. The page identifies MSR as one of Cascade Designs’ brands and says approved applicants receive custom links. This supports MSR stoves, shelters, water treatment, and snow equipment as a direct publisher path after approval.

BioLite’s official publisher page states that its program is managed through Partnerize and explicitly invites video, article, and review creators to apply. It states that approved publishers can create hyperlinks and may qualify for product sends based on reach and performance. This supports BioLite’s premium off-grid lighting, fire, power, and cooking systems after approval.

Sources: Cascade Designs, https://cascadedesigns.com/pages/cascade-designs-affiliate; BioLite, https://www.bioliteenergy.com/pages/biolite-affiliate-program.

## Verified Black Diamond and Osprey Paths

Black Diamond’s official affiliate page directs applicants to AvantLink and states that content creators receive a base commission, creative assets, a dedicated affiliate manager, and a 30-day cookie. This supports high-end climbing, avalanche, ski-touring, and mountain hardware research entries after approval.

Osprey’s official affiliate-marketing page provides an application route and describes affiliate benefits. The page is a valid publisher pathway for premium pack and travel-system entries; exact terms should be reviewed in the owner’s approval account before any link is activated.

Sources: Black Diamond, https://blackdiamondequipment.com/pages/dd-affiliate-program; Osprey, https://www.osprey.com/culture/affiliate.

## Premium Satellite Connectivity Routes

Connecta Satellite Solutions, operating the Satellite Phone Store affiliate registration page, publishes a United States publisher application for satellite phones and plans. The page states that an approved site URL is required and that affiliates receive links and banners after approval. It is therefore a viable direct route for premium Iridium and satellite-phone research after the owner is approved. Its terms prohibit resale-site promotion and paid search or social ads, so links must remain editorial website or creator content links and should never be placed on a resale marketplace.

Starlink’s official Roam page supports the editorial topic of mobile satellite internet, but the research did not identify a public official Starlink publisher affiliate application. Starlink Roam should therefore remain an **editorial-only research topic** until an official partner path is confirmed. Do not use a third-party “Starlink affiliate” listing as a substitute for Starlink’s own approval.

Sources: Connecta Satellite Solutions, https://satellitephonestore.refersion.com/affiliate/registration; Starlink, https://starlink.com/roam.

## Confirmed Goal Zero Path

Goal Zero’s official affiliate page invites individual creators and businesses to apply and states that the program is managed through Partnerize. It describes access to text links and an automated product feed after participation. This verifies the direct publisher path used for Goal Zero’s high-end Yeti and solar-system research entries.

Source: Goal Zero, https://goalzero.com/pages/affiliate-marketing-program.

## Catalog Rule

Every product in the expanded high-end watchlist must carry a non-public `affiliatePath` status. Valid statuses are **direct merchant program—application required**, **premium retailer program—application required**, or **no verified path—do not monetize**. No public card may claim a discount, commission, price, availability, or retailer relationship. Only approved tracking links may later be activated, with the sitewide and near-link disclosures already in place.
