# Research Notes: Launch Catalog

## Outdoor Exoskeletons

Hypershell positions its X Series for hiking, cycling, photography, ski touring, rescue, inspection, and security applications. Its hiking page emphasizes fatigue reduction, adaptive support, extended-use battery capability, and real-time movement assistance; these are manufacturer claims that must be clearly attributed in site copy.

Outdoor Life field-tested the Hypershell Pro X and Dnsys X1 Carbon. Its 2025 comparison listed the products at $960 and $1,000, respectively, with listed weights of 4.4 lb and 3.5 lb. The review found useful movement sensing in casual terrain while also noting altered gait, discomfort, greater foot impact, and a period of recalibration after use. Gear Guru Guide should frame this category as an emerging, high-consideration assistive technology rather than as medical equipment or a universal hiking upgrade.

## Editorial Implication

The first exoskeleton guide should use a transparent decision framework: intended terrain, user fitness and experience, pack load, battery logistics, comfort and fit, warranty/support, safety limits, and independent field testing. It must distinguish manufacturer claims from verified field observations and carry an affiliate disclosure wherever monetized links appear.

## Premium Expedition Power

EcoFlow positions the DELTA Pro 3 as a portable station for RV and off-grid energy needs. The manufacturer lists expandable capacity of 4–48 kWh, a 4,000 W output, 120/240 V output, LFP battery chemistry rated to retain 80% capacity after 4,000 cycles, and solar/car/outlet charging. These specifications are manufacturer-stated and should be checked against the current product page before publication.

## Amazon Affiliate Data Path

Amazon’s current documentation presents the Creators API as its REST-based product-catalog interface. It documents SearchItems, GetItems, GetVariations, and GetBrowseNodes operations. Access requires enrollment in the relevant Associates marketplace, at least 10 qualifying sales in the prior 30 days for PA API access through Creators API, registration in Associates Central, and generated credentials. This makes it unsuitable as a day-one automatic catalog source for a brand-new Associates account; the site should launch with editorially selected products and owner-supplied compliant links, then activate the live catalog importer after eligibility is established.

## Affiliate Disclosure

The launch site needs a clear disclosure near monetized links and a sitewide disclosure page. Amazon’s policies require public, original content and correctly formatted Special Links tied to the assigned Associates ID. The website should not cache or manually maintain product price, availability, images, or rating content until an approved API workflow is implemented and validated against the current license terms.

## Faceless Video Production

Higgsfield’s Cinema Studio is positioned around camera controls and scene-by-scene creation. Its prompt workflow asks creators to define subject, style, camera, lighting, mood, aspect ratio, references, shot lists, output length, and variants. The initial Gear Guru Guide prompts should therefore be organized as a short shot list rather than a single generic paragraph. They will use a recurring visual language of gloved hands, boot-level movement, equipment details, topographic textures, and atmospheric landscapes, with no presenter face and no unverified product performance claims.
